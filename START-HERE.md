# Start here

This is a public, sanitized copy of the live BMC workspace.

**BMC (Build Market Close) is the sales layer for people who build.** You learned to ship
anything with folders, markdown, and 1 good model. This workspace is the other half: the
journey from "I have nothing to sell" to "I closed a deal," structured the same way you
already work. It is an ICM about selling, built by someone who sold for 25 years before
teaching a line of it.

## Who wrote this

Ryan McKinney. Marine, started selling at 15, first online sale in 2006, 10,000+ customers
on a list he still owns, featured on Entrepreneurs On Fire. Every framework in here was run
by hand first, then taught, then packaged. Nothing is theory.

  one.

## How to read it

1. `CLAUDE.md` is the map and the hard rules.
2. `CONTEXT.md` is the router: match your task, go to 1 home, stop.
3. `_config/` is the factory: the thesis, the journey, and the constraints every surface obeys.
4. `_reference/curriculum/` is the doctrine, and it opens the way the training does:
   `the-hat.md` (what sales is and what it isn't) comes FIRST, then the 7-stage sales
   framework, the 3 objection handlers, the cold call framework, and the advanced close,
   all in Ryan's exact words. Each file's header states whether it is confirmed canon or
   awaiting its confirmation pass.
   A note for the person starting from absolute zero: the free training's own Start Here
   holds Ryan's story, the public skills, and the get-Claude video (free vs pro, and why
   everything he gives away runs on a FREE Claude account). You do not need a paid AI to
   begin.
5. The numbered stage folders are the product's spine. An absent `output/` folder is the
   status "not started." That is honesty, not neglect.

## What this copy strips, and why

The live workspace also carries a price ladder, an infrastructure inventory, a decision log,
a beta plan, a content ledger, and a rolling state log. All of it is removed here because it
is operational: real account ids, real people, and prices that are deliberately undecided.
Nothing in this copy references those files, so what you are reading is internally complete.

If you are auditing this workspace: code what is here. The strips above are disclosed so an
absent file reads as a removal, not a claim.
