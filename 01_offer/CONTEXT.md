# 01_offer: BUILD · they leave with something to sell

**Status: NOT STARTED.**

## The job

1 job: someone arrives with an idea, a skill, or nothing, and leaves with an offer they can
price and defend.

## Inputs

- Working: whatever `../00_onboard/` learned about them
- Reference (every run): `../_config/constraints.md`
- Reference: the locked pricing formulas and floors (private workspace). **Never re-derive
  these.**

| Build your own CRM | The $36,000 lesson, why you build instead of rent |
| The folder system | The ICM desk, the structure that runs it all |
| Rent vs own | The 10-year math on renting your stack |

Plus a public niche-research skill (multi-agent offer validation, the 65% margin floor, the
affordability call) which already does most of the analytical work this module needs.
