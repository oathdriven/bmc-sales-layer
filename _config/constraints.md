## BANNED

**Say this, not that:**

| Say | Never say | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **training** | course | *"Being trained sounds better than taking a course."* A course is homework you bought. Training is something being done to you by someone who has done it. It also implies a trainer, which is the whole point. |
| customers who say yes | leads, prospects (in customer-facing copy) | |
| nothing | **buy**, **sell** | Removed from the fold on purpose. Force everything into outcomes instead. |

**Copy:**
- **No em dashes.** Anywhere. Including AI output, including this workspace.
- **Numbers always as digits.** Never spelled out, except money at the moment of a close.
- No "unlock / supercharge / game changer / secret / hack / 10x."
- No countdowns, no fake scarcity, no exclamation points in a headline or a CTA.
- No standard sales vocabulary on builder-facing surfaces. The research is explicit: they
  never say pipeline, prospecting, discovery call, qualify, objection handling, closing,
  quota, value proposition, or book a demo. *"A closer's page has to be written without a
  closer's words."*
- No "first paying client in 90 days or your money back." 5 competitors run it verbatim.
- No naming anything OS / Vault / Stack / Starter Kit / Accelerator / Lab / Hub / Room.

**Product:**
- **Never invent a number.** Every figure traces to a source or carries a MODELED tag with the
  arithmetic shown. This rule exists because it was violated once: an invented value stack
  reached a live page before it was caught.
- **Never invent proof.** If a section needs a testimonial or a result that does not exist,
  mark it a gap and say so out loud.
- **The math is deterministic TypeScript, never an LLM.** The LLM writes connective tissue
  around verbatim lines and never paraphrases them.
- **Ryan's lines are verbatim-only.** The generator inserts them as untouchable tokens.
- **No price anywhere in this workspace.** See hard rule 2 in `CLAUDE.md`.

**Do not inherit:**
- **The terminal aesthetic.** The teaching brand keeps it; its audience is developers. BMC
  sells to someone who may never have opened a terminal.
- **Any sibling brand's palette.** BMC is its own visual identity.
