# 02_gameplan: MARKET · they leave with a plan and traffic

**Status: 1 SLICE IS IN V1, the cold call plan. Everything else waits.**

## The v1 slice

Only 1 thing from this module ships in v1: **the cold call plan.** Goals in, real numbers
out. *X dials a day, this script, the goal of the call is the appointment, and here is what a
booked appointment is worth to you.* The math is deterministic code that already exists.

It ships because it sits directly in front of the cold call reps, which are the v1 wedge. A
rep with no plan behind it is a video game.

Everything else in this module defers.

**THIS IS STILL THE THIN MODULE. Read the warning below.**

## The job

1 job: someone has an offer nobody knows about, and leaves with a machine that fills a
calendar. Not a good month. A machine.

## The warning

**MARKET is a folder name, not a curriculum.** Of roughly 20 catalogued content assets:

- about 10 are CLOSE
- about 7 are BUILD
- **2 are genuinely MARKET**

**Build this module's content before building its software.** Designing a 3-stage product
where stage 2 has 2 assets is how a journey turns into a promise the classroom cannot keep.

Note: the lead-generation lesson lives in the sales classroom **on purpose**, per THE LAW
("once a lead is generated, it is a sales problem"). Reaching out to a generated lead takes
sales copy, so that teaching is sales, not marketing.
